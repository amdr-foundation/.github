# Code of conduct

AMDR Foundation repositories are learning spaces. Everyone who takes part, learners, instructors, volunteers and contributors, agrees to:

- Be respectful. No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Assume good faith. Many contributors are new to automation and to GitHub.
- Keep learner data private. Never commit names, emails, phone numbers, or credentials.
- Credit sources and respect licences.

Report concerns to alicia@amdrfoundation.org. The Foundation may remove content or contributors who break these rules.
