# AMDR Foundation Inc

A 501(c)(3) public charity in Atlanta, Georgia, connecting underserved communities, especially women and small business owners, with the skills, tools and opportunities to build successful, scalable ventures. We deliver in Atlanta and in Kingston, Jamaica.

**Programs**
- **EmpowerTech: AI Workflow Automation with n8n**: an 8-week, 30-hour instructor-led course for adult learners and enterprise teams. Course repository: [empowertech-n8n-curriculum](https://github.com/amdr-foundation/empowertech-n8n-curriculum)
- **n8n community workshops**: Atlanta and Kingston meetups led by our founder, an n8n Ambassador
- **The Economics of Things** and **Financial Literacy**: plain-language video series on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@amdrfoundationorg)
